Short Bark & Sides was founded by Collette Sykes in 2010, built on a lifelong love of dogs and the belief that grooming should feel personal, calm and caring.

With a particular passion for hand stripping and individual attention, Collette welcomes pups of all shapes and sizes to her Meanwood studio.

Good to know

Questions?
We've got
your tail.

Need something else? Give the salon a ring — we're happy to help you find the right groom.

Do you groom all dog breeds?

Yes, the salon states that it caters for all breeds, from small terriers to larger dogs.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Grooming is by advance appointment so each dog receives individual attention.

Can I book hand stripping?

Yes. Collette specialises in hand stripping wire-haired breeds. Ask about your dog's coat when enquiring.

Are the prices fixed?

No. Published prices are starting guides; the final price depends on your dog's coat and needs.
